# The shippable model is English-first; multilingual stays the science track

The artifact we release covers English, because that is where the data, the tooling and every
published comparison live. The multilingual corpus, the byte-phase finding across scripts, and
the vision and audio arms continue as the science track and are not dropped — they are simply
not what the shippable model is.

Consequence: effort splits deliberately rather than by accident, and any claim about non-Latin
scripts comes from the science track rather than from the released checkpoint.
