# One write-up, with model quality as the headline

The byte-phase finding stays as a section of a single write-up whose headline is the model.
Splitting it into a mechanism paper and a model paper was rejected because the mechanism result
is most interesting *as* the explanation for the model's behaviour, and splitting them would
hide that link.

Consequence: the existing write-up in docs/WRITEUP.md is restructured rather than replaced, and
the mechanism section leads into the model-quality story rather than standing alone.
